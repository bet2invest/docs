### Technical rules <a href="#technical-rules" id="technical-rules"></a>

* All predictions published on the Site must be **original content, exclusive** to the Site.
* The odds provided in real time by Bet2invest are **exclusively** those of the international bookmaker **Pinnacle** (pinnacle.com).
* Any analysis (optional) accompanying a prediction must be unique, relevant and sufficiently substantiated to support that specific prediction.

**Eligible competition:**

* ⚽ Football — **2026 World Cup**

**Prediction conditions:**

* The allowed stake is a **single unit (1 unit)**.
* Eligible matches are those of the 2026 World Cup, both **pre-match and Live**.
* Once published, a prediction **cannot be edited**.
* Only one prediction is allowed **per market and per event** (e.g., one 1X2 prediction and one Over/Under prediction per match; you cannot predict the 1X2, or the Over/Under, of the same event more than once).
* Predictions must be made before the event's kick-off, or Live.

**Disputes and settlement:**

* In the event of a dispute, the **prediction's description prevails**. Always make sure it clearly reflects your intent.
* When a prediction's settlement is disputed, the final decision is based on how **Pinnacle** settled the bet on its site. Accordingly, if a bet is voided by the bookmaker, the corresponding prediction is also voided on Bet2invest.

### Determining the winners <a href="#determining-the-winners" id="determining-the-winners"></a>

* To be eligible, a Tipster must have **at least 35 settled predictions** (at 1 unit) on 2026 World Cup matches during the Contest period.
* The ranking is based on a **composite score**, calculated automatically by our own tools and recomputed throughout the competition (winning and losing predictions alike). It combines three metrics, **normalized across all eligible participants** (each metric is rescaled to a 0–100 range, then weighted):

| Metric                       | Weight |
| ---------------------------- | ------ |
| **Yield**                    | 50%    |
| **CLV** (Closing Line Value) | 40%    |
| **Volume** of predictions    | 10%    |

* **Volume** is capped: beyond a certain number of predictions, publishing more no longer increases your score (anti-spam measure).
* At the end of the competition cycle, the final leaderboard determines how the prizes are awarded: tipsters are ranked from the highest to the lowest score. The **Top 10** is rewarded.
* **In the event of a tie**, the tie-break order is as follows: best **yield**, then best **CLV**, then **earliest enrollment** in the Contest.

### Breach of the rules <a href="#breach-of-the-rules" id="breach-of-the-rules"></a>

All content and comments published on the Site must remain courteous. Respect your fellow tipsters and refrain from any abusive, racist, sexist, homophobic language or any other form of discrimination. We also ask that you avoid any intentionally provocative content (for example, extreme political opinions). The Site is for everyone, whoever they are, wherever they come from and whatever they believe.

Any attempt to exploit the Site's system is prohibited and will result in sanctions.

Copying someone else's work to publish it under your own name, intentionally exploiting bugs, or attempting in any way to gain an unfair advantage over other tipsters is strictly unacceptable. Predictions are verified, and any attempt to cheat or circumvent the rules will be severely sanctioned. Conversely, honesty and respect for the Site's integrity are valued and often rewarded.

When administrators correct predictions that breach the rules above, they remove all **predictions** that generated a profit for the tipster. Losing predictions, however, are kept, since they result from the tipster's failure to comply with the rules.

The Organizer reserves the right to reward or sanction tipsters whenever it deems it justified. An explanation is always provided when a tipster acts outside the normal rules of the competition.

Sanctions include the cancellation or removal of predictions, warnings, temporary suspensions and, in certain circumstances, a permanent ban. The extent of a sanction depends on the severity of the offense and whether the tipster is a repeat offender. Each case is judged on its own merits; the Organizer's decisions are final and must be respected.

Any issue or situation not explicitly covered by these Rules will be handled at the discretion of the Site's administration.
