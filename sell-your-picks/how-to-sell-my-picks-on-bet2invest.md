# How to sell my picks on Bet2Invest

Bet2Invest allows any tipster to monetize their expertise by selling their picks directly on the platform. This guide explains how to unlock the Paid status, set up your pricing, and start earning.

***

### Paid vs. PRO: What's the difference? <a href="#paid-vs-pro--whats-the-difference" id="paid-vs-pro--whats-the-difference"></a>

Bet2Invest has two paid statuses for tipsters:

|                    | **Paid**                                          | **PRO**                                        |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **How you get it** | Self-activated (one-time fee)                     | Granted by Bet2Invest based on performance     |
| **Pricing**        | You set your own prices                           | Prices set automatically by Bet2Invest         |
| **Certification**  | Not certified — you commit to the Tipster Charter | Certified by Bet2Invest                        |
| **Visibility**     | Visible on the platform                           | Featured and promoted on the platform          |
| **Requirements**   | Accept the Charter + pay the activation fee       | Meet performance thresholds (ROI, pick volume) |

> **Important:** The Paid status does not imply endorsement by Bet2Invest. Subscribers are informed that Paid tipsters are independent and that Bet2Invest does not guarantee their results.

***

### Step 1: Accept the Tipster Charter <a href="#step-1--accept-the-tipster-charter" id="step-1--accept-the-tipster-charter"></a>

Before activating, you must read and accept the **Tipster Charter**. Key commitments include:

* Publish at least **15 picks per month** (for subscription-based selling)
* Maintain **stable stake sizing** across your picks
* Provide **well-reasoned picks** based on a defined strategy
* **Never copy** picks from other tipsters
* Accept that your status can be **revoked** if you violate the rules, with no refund

***

### Step 2: Pay the one-time activation fee <a href="#step-2--pay-the-one-time-activation-fee" id="step-2--pay-the-one-time-activation-fee"></a>

A one-time, non-refundable payment of **€25** is required to unlock the Paid status. This fee ensures a serious commitment from tipsters who wish to sell their picks.

The amount is automatically converted to your local currency based on your location.

***

### Step 3: Set your subscription price <a href="#step-3--set-your-subscription-price" id="step-3--set-your-subscription-price"></a>

Once the payment is confirmed, you need to set your **monthly subscription price**:

* **Minimum:** €10/month
* **Maximum:** €500/month

Your subscription allows followers to access all your picks for the duration of their subscription.

> Your status remains **inactive** until you set a price. No one can subscribe to you until this step is complete.

### Step 4: Configure pick packs (optional) <a href="#step-4--configure-pick-packs-optional" id="step-4--configure-pick-packs-optional"></a>

In addition to subscriptions, you can offer **pick packs**; bundles of a fixed number of picks at a set price.

#### Pack rules <a href="#pack-rules" id="pack-rules"></a>

* You can create up to **7 different packs**
* Price per pick must be between **€0.20** and **€5.00**
* Larger packs must have a **lower or equal price per pick** than smaller packs (volume discount logic)
* Minimum pack price: **€1**

### Step 5: Manage your status <a href="#step-5--manage-your-status" id="step-5--manage-your-status"></a>

From your account settings (_Sell My Picks_ tab), you can:

* **Activate / Deactivate** your entire paid status, _disabling it cancels all active subscriber subscriptions_
* **Toggle subscriptions on/off** independently from pick packs
* **Toggle pick packs on/off** independently from subscriptions
* **Update your prices** at any time

> At least one of subscriptions or packs must remain active. You cannot disable both simultaneously.

#### What happens when you deactivate? <a href="#what-happens-when-you-deactivate" id="what-happens-when-you-deactivate"></a>

* Your status switches to **FREE**
* All active subscriptions are **canceled**
* You **keep your unlock**, you can re-enable at any time without paying the €25 fee again

### Upgrade From Paid to PRO

When your account meets the criteria to be classified as "PRO" on B2I, it will automatically be upgraded to a "PRO" account.

If your prices are below the PRO standards:\
\- Your prices will be adjusted to meet the PRO standards.

If your prices are higher than what the PRO level offers and you have subscribers:\
\- Your prices will remain unchanged.

If you do not have subscribers, your prices will be reduced. If you wish to maintain your current prices, please contact the Bet2Invest team.

***

### Revenue & Payouts <a href="#revenue--payouts" id="revenue--payouts"></a>

Bet2Invest takes a commission on each payment received from your subscribers.

* Prices are configured in **EUR** and automatically converted to the subscriber's currency
* You have the choice for the payouts between: Bank Transfer, Cryptocurrencies or PayPal.

***

### Revocation <a href="#revocation" id="revocation"></a>

An administrator can **permanently revoke** your Paid status if you violate the Tipster Charter. Revocation:

* Cancels all active subscriptions
* Permanently restricts your account from re-activating the Paid status
* Is **not refundable**
