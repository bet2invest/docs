# Why Go Paid?

The Paid status exists for one reason: **let you start earning immediately, without waiting months to qualify for PRO.**

***

### The problem it solves <a href="#the-problem-it-solves" id="the-problem-it-solves"></a>

To become a PRO tipster on Bet2Invest, you need to meet strict performance criteria - including a minimum of **400 tracked picks on Pinnacle** with a positive ROI. For most tipsters, that means months of free activity before earning a single euro.

This is a real barrier for two types of people:

#### Tipsters who already have a community <a href="#tipsters-who-already-have-a-community" id="tipsters-who-already-have-a-community"></a>

You may already be selling picks on Telegram, Discord, or another platform. You have followers, you have a track record - but on Bet2Invest, you're starting from zero. The Paid status lets you **bring your audience to B2I and start monetizing immediately** while you build your on-platform history.

#### Tipsters who don't want to wait <a href="#tipsters-who-dont-want-to-wait" id="tipsters-who-dont-want-to-wait"></a>

Maybe you're confident in your analysis and don't want to spend 6 months posting for free before you can charge. The Paid status lets you **skip the queue** and start selling from day one, while your picks accumulate toward PRO eligibility.

***

### Paid is the path to PRO <a href="#paid-is-the-path-to-pro" id="paid-is-the-path-to-pro"></a>

The Paid status is not the destination - it's the **fast lane to PRO**.

Here's how it works in practice:

1. You activate Paid and start selling picks immediately
2. Your picks are tracked on Bet2Invest just like any other tipster
3. Once you hit the PRO criteria (ROI, pick volume, consistency), Bet2Invest upgrades you automatically

Consider this new "Paid" status as a **way to monetize your picks while waiting to go PRO**, rather than staying on the bench.

***

### What PRO gives you that Paid doesn't <a href="#what-pro-gives-you-that-paid-doesnt" id="what-pro-gives-you-that-paid-doesnt"></a>

Once you reach PRO, you unlock significant advantages:

|                         | **Paid**                     | **PRO**                                                                      |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Commission**          | Standard rate                | Reduced commission - you keep more                                           |
| **B2I Pro Tools**       | Not included                 | Full access for free (opening odds notifications, and at lot more coming...) |
| **Platform visibility** | Listed like any tipster      | Featured placement and promotion by Bet2Invest                               |
| **Certification badge** | No certification             | Certified PRO badge - builds trust instantly                                 |
| **Pricing**             | You set your own prices      | Optimized pricing set by Bet2Invest based on your performance                |
| **Subscriber trust**    | Warning shown to subscribers | Endorsed by Bet2Invest: no warning                                           |

In short: **PRO tipsters earn more, are seen by more people, and convert subscribers at a higher rate** because the platform actively promotes them.

***

### The typical journey <a href="#the-typical-journey" id="the-typical-journey"></a>

```
Free → Paid → PRO
```

1. **Free:** You join Bet2Invest, post your picks, build a track record
2. **Paid:** You activate selling early: your picks keep counting toward PRO
3. **PRO:** Bet2Invest recognizes your performance and upgrades you automatically

***

### Who should NOT go Paid <a href="#who-should-not-go-paid" id="who-should-not-go-paid"></a>

The Paid status is not for everyone. If you:

* Have no track record at all and haven't analyzed sports seriously
* Expect guaranteed income without consistent effort
* Are not willing to publish at least 15 picks per month

Then the Paid status is probably not for you yet. Focus on building a free track record first, learn how the platform works, and activate Paid when you're ready to commit seriously.

***

### Key takeaway <a href="#key-takeaway" id="key-takeaway"></a>

The Paid status is a **bridge, not a ceiling.** It lets you start earning while you work toward PRO — where the real advantages are. The sooner you start building your track record on Bet2Invest, the sooner you'll get there.
