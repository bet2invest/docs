# Paid Tipster Charter

Every tipster who wishes to sell picks on Bet2Invest must read, understand, and accept the following charter before activating their Paid status. This charter exists to protect subscribers and maintain the quality and integrity of the platform.

***

### Your commitments <a href="#your-commitments" id="your-commitments"></a>

By activating the Paid status, you agree to the following:

#### 1. Respect the Terms of Service <a href="#id-1-respect-the-terms-of-service" id="id-1-respect-the-terms-of-service"></a>

You commit to respecting Bet2Invest's general terms of use and terms of sale at all times. Violation of any platform rule may lead to the revocation of your Paid status.

#### 2. Publish regularly <a href="#id-2-publish-regularly" id="id-2-publish-regularly"></a>

You commit to publishing **at least 15 picks per month** on Bet2Invest. This requirement applies to subscription-based selling - subscribers expect consistent activity from the tipsters they pay for.

> If you need a break, use the **Season Break** feature to temporarily pause your activity without losing your status.

#### 3. Maintain stable staking <a href="#id-3-maintain-stable-staking" id="id-3-maintain-stable-staking"></a>

You commit to maintaining **stable and consistent stake sizing** across your picks. Erratic staking (e.g., suddenly betting 10 units after a losing streak) undermines trust and is grounds for review.

#### 4. Provide reasoned picks <a href="#id-4-provide-reasoned-picks" id="id-4-provide-reasoned-picks"></a>

You commit to providing **well-thought-out picks** based on a defined strategy. Picks should reflect genuine analysis, not random selections. You are not required to share your reasoning publicly, but your track record should reflect a coherent approach.

#### 5. Accept the pricing policy <a href="#id-5-accept-the-pricing-policy" id="id-5-accept-the-pricing-policy"></a>

You accept Bet2Invest's pricing policy, including:

* Subscription prices between **€10 and €500/month**
* Pick pack prices with a per-pick cost between **€0.20 and €5.00**
* Bet2Invest's commission on all subscriber payments.

#### 6. Understand that your status can be revoked <a href="#id-6-understand-that-your-status-can-be-revoked" id="id-6-understand-that-your-status-can-be-revoked"></a>

You understand that your Paid status may be **permanently revoked** by Bet2Invest administrators if you fail to respect the rules outlined in this charter. Revocation cancels all active subscriptions and permanently restricts your account.

#### 7. Never copy other tipsters <a href="#id-7-never-copy-other-tipsters" id="id-7-never-copy-other-tipsters"></a>

Copying picks from other tipsters - whether on Bet2Invest or external platforms - is **strictly prohibited** and will result in immediate and permanent revocation of your Paid status.

#### 8. No refund upon revocation <a href="#id-8-no-refund-upon-revocation" id="id-8-no-refund-upon-revocation"></a>

You accept that **no refund** of the activation fee will be issued if your Paid status is revoked for non-compliance with this charter.

***

### What Bet2Invest guarantees <a href="#what-bet2invest-guarantees" id="what-bet2invest-guarantees"></a>

* **Fair enforcement:** Rules are applied consistently to all Paid tipsters
* **Transparency:** You will be informed of the reason for any action taken on your account
* **Tools to succeed:** Access to pricing settings, analytics, and subscriber management tools
* **Subscriber protection:** Subscribers are clearly informed that Paid tipsters are not certified by Bet2Invest and that results are not guaranteed

***

### What Bet2Invest does NOT guarantee <a href="#what-bet2invest-does-not-guarantee" id="what-bet2invest-does-not-guarantee"></a>

* Your profitability or the profitability of your subscribers
* A minimum number of subscribers or revenue
* Protection against negative reviews or subscriber churn
